Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is an IPTV Service Provider and how does it work?

An IPTV Service Provider delivers television content through internet protocol networks instead of traditional cable or satellite. You stream live channels, movies, and shows via an app on your smart TV, phone, tablet, or computer. All you need is a stable internet connection (minimum 10 Mbps recommended for HD streaming).

2. Is IPTV legal?

Yes, IPTV technology itself is completely legal. It's simply a method of delivering TV content over the internet. However, you should ensure you're subscribing to legitimate services that have proper licensing agreements with content providers. Our service operates within legal frameworks and provides licensed content.

3. What devices are compatible with your IPTV service?

Our IPTV works on virtually all modern devices including: Smart TVs (Samsung, LG, Sony, Android TV), Amazon Firestick, Android TV boxes, smartphones (Android and iOS), tablets, laptops, Windows/Mac computers, and dedicated IPTV set-top boxes. One subscription typically allows multiple device connections.

4. How much internet speed do I need for smooth streaming?

For standard definition (SD) content, 5-10 Mbps is sufficient. For HD quality, we recommend at least 15-25 Mbps. For 4K Ultra HD streaming, 25-50 Mbps is ideal. Most broadband connections easily meet these requirements. Our adaptive streaming technology also adjusts quality based on your connection speed to prevent buffering.
